CARTOGRAPHIC ANALYSIS

SPATIAL PATTERN RECOGNITION

Inner-City Densification & Topographic Polarization

Zurich’s demographic distribution exhibits acute spatial concentration shaped by two defining physical barriers: Lake Zurich (Zürichsee) and the steep elevational relief of the Uetliberg and Zürichberg massifs.

Municipal planning strategies over the past two decades have directed infill growth along the central rail corridors running through Aussersihl (Kreis 4) and Industriequartier (Kreis 5). The resulting density gradient requires a non-linear classification framework to prevent the dilution of high-density nodes into broad regional generalizations.

Cartographic Legibility Standards

  • 01. Hydrographic contrast using calibrated blue tints to clearly separate the Limmat river corridor and lake boundary.
  • 02. Subdued topographic hillshade in the peripheral southwestern quadrant to depict uninhabited forest reserves without clutter.
  • 03. Perceptually sequential color ramp graduating from light teal (<2k pop/km²) into deep midnight indigo (>12k pop/km²).

STRATIFIED DENSITY TIERS (JENKS)

TIER 1: >12,000 POP / KM² PEAK INTENSITY

Kreis 4 (Aussersihl), Langstrasse corridor, Hardbrücke junction.

TIER 2: 7,000 – 12,000 POP / KM² URBAN CORE

Kreis 3 (Wiedikon), Kreis 5 (Industriequartier), Kreis 6 (Unterstrass).

TIER 3: 2,000 – 7,000 POP / KM² RESIDENTIAL

Kreis 10 (Höngg/Wipkingen), Kreis 11 (Oerlikon/Affoltern), Kreis 2 (Wollishofen).

TIER 4: <2,000 POP / KM² PERIPHERAL

Kreis 7 (Zürichberg slopes, Fluntern, Witikon), Uetliberg buffer zones.

# Zurich Spatial Demographics ETL & Choropleth Binner

# ------------------------------------------------------------------------
import geopandas as gpd
import pandas as pd
import mapclassify as mc

# 1. Ingest official municipal boundaries (Stadt Zürich Open Data)
gdf_kreise = gpd.read_file("data/raw/stadt_zuerich_statistische_quartiere.shp").to_crs(epsg=2056)

# 2. Join 2024 annual population census records
df_pop = pd.read_csv("data/raw/bevoelkerung_pro_kreis_2024.csv")
gdf_merged = gdf_kreise.merge(df_pop, on="KREIS_NR", how="left")

# 3. Calculate spatial area in km² and standardized density metric
gdf_merged["area_km2"] = gdf_merged.geometry.area / 1_000_000.0
gdf_merged["density_km2"] = gdf_merged["total_pop"] / gdf_merged["area_km2"]

# 4. Compute Jenks Natural Breaks classification (k=5 classes)
classifier = mc.NaturalBreaks(gdf_merged["density_km2"], k=5)
gdf_merged["jenks_bin"] = classifier.yb

# 5. Export clean vector dataset for QGIS print layout styling
gdf_merged.to_file("data/processed/zuerich_density_2024_classified.geojson", driver="GeoJSON")
print(f"Processing complete. Breaks: {classifier.bins}")
